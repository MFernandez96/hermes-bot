Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who uses this app

Hermes Bot is a personal app used only by its owner. It is not available to the public.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app uses the Google Sheets and Google Drive APIs to create spreadsheets in the owner's Google Drive and to read and append rows to them. It does not access Gmail, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to record the owner's own daily entries (for example meals, expenses and workouts) in the owner's own spreadsheets.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's private server.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
